# Store screenshots

These are unmodified captures of Kvil's production SwiftUI views using fictional schedules and reflections. English and Norwegian iPhone images are 1320 × 2868. Watch images are 410 × 502 in both languages. The purchase review image uses Apple's local StoreKit test catalog at US $3.99.

`manifest.json` records each file, dimensions, and SHA-256. The seven iPhone images and purchase review image were refreshed on 13 September 2026 after replacing the painted landscapes with Kayvan Mazhar's Rice Lake photograph. These eight current files have not been uploaded to Apple. The two Watch captures are unchanged because the Watch screen contains no landscape.

`../apple-media-verified.json` is the historical delivery proof for the previous set of ten images. It records Apple asset IDs and COMPLETE states for that earlier upload, not the refreshed iPhone and purchase files. Replace those eight Apple assets alongside the next archive; do not treat the old checksums as verification of this manifest. `../photography-verified.json` records the new captures' test run and visual checks.

The iPhone images belong to the 6.9-inch display class in App Store Connect. The Watch screenshots are captured on the Ultra 2 simulator. Smaller-phone and 42-mm Watch layout checks are separate QA captures under `build/`.

Apple's current [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications) accept these dimensions. Apple accepted the iPhone captures in APP_IPHONE_67 (displayed as 6.9-inch in the current UI) and the Watch captures in APP_WATCH_ULTRA.
